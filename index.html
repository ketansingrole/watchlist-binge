Privacy Policy

Watchlist Binge — Last updated: August 28, 2026

Summary: Watchlist Binge does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All your watchlist data stays on your device.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Watchlist Binge ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our iOS app Watchlist Binge.

We designed Watchlist Binge to respect your privacy. We do not collect any personal data.

Data Not Collected

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Contact information, location, identifiers, or usage data
  • Diagnostics or analytics data
  • Any data linked to your identity

As stated in App Store Connect > App Privacy: Data Not Collected.

Data Stored on Device

Your movies, shows, and watchlist preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (including iCloud sync if you have enabled it in your device settings). This data never goes to our servers — we don't have servers that receive it.

Third-Party Services

Watchlist Binge does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

If the app displays information about movies/shows (e.g., titles, posters), that information is fetched from public catalog APIs. Those requests are not associated with your personal identity and we do not send them any personal data.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at this URL.
