EPISODES

Know exactly where you left off.

Every show keeps its own progress, season by season. Tick off an episode or a whole season, and see how much is left before you start.

  • Progress on every poster, so the half-finished shows are easy to spot
  • Time remaining for a season, and the time you'd finish if you started now
  • Mark all watched for a season or a whole series you caught up on elsewhere
  • Unwatched and Watched kept apart, with sorting and filters for both
DETAILS

Decide what's next without leaving the app.

Each movie and show has its trailers, cast, ratings and runtime on one page, and you can add it to your list from anywhere you find it.

  • Trailers play right on the page
  • Where to stream it, so you know before you sit down
  • Cast and crew, each with their own page of credits
  • Light or dark, whichever you watch in
EVERYTHING ELSE

Built for people who watch a lot.

New-episode alerts

An alert when the next episode of a show you follow airs. Turn it off per show.

Share a list over iCloud with a partner or friends. You're told who added or watched what.

Upcoming widget

What's releasing next from your list, right on your Home Screen.

Discover

What's trending and coming soon, for the country you choose.

Stars

Siri and Shortcuts

Add a title, mark an episode or open your list with your voice or a Shortcut.

PRIVATE

Your list is nobody's business.

There's no Watchlist Binge account and no Watchlist Binge server. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

No sign-up

Open the app and start adding. Nothing to register, nothing to remember.

Your iCloud

Your library syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics or advertising code in the app, and nothing sold or shared.

Delete it any time

Settings can wipe the library from a device before you hand it to someone else.

The details are in the privacy policy.

SUPPORT

Need a hand?

Most questions come down to one of these. If yours doesn't, email and you'll hear back from the person who builds the app.

Contact

Email singroleketan@gmail.com with your device, the app version (Settings › About) and what happened.

iCloud sync

Sign into iCloud on each device to sync your library. Titles added while signed out stay on the device and upload when you sign back in.

Notifications

Turn on alerts for a show in the app. If they don't arrive, allow notifications for Watchlist Binge in your device's Settings.
