Privacy Policy

Watchlist Binge — Last updated: October 30, 2026

Summary: We do not collect, store, or share your personal data, and we don't run servers that receive it. Your library stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. Lists you choose to share are visible to the people you share them with.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Watchlist Binge ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Watchlist Binge app on iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, receive, or store any of the following:

  • Contact information, location, or device identifiers
  • Usage, diagnostics, or analytics data
  • Your watchlist, search history, or any data linked to your identity

The app has no sign-up, no advertising, and no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Our App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Data Stored on Your Device and in iCloud

Your movies, shows, watch progress, and preferences are stored on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, this data syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud account, which Apple operates under its own privacy policy. We cannot access it.

Shared Lists

If you create or join a shared list, the list's titles and activity, along with your iCloud display name, are shared through iCloud with the people on that list. Those people can see this information, and the app may send them notifications about changes to the list. Only share lists with people you trust. You can stop sharing or leave a list at any time. We cannot see shared lists.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. The app only asks for permission when you turn on alerts, and you can turn them off at any time in your device's Settings.

Third-Party Services

To show movie and TV information, the app connects directly to these services:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) for titles, posters, cast, search results, and streaming availability (provided by JustWatch through TMDB)
  • TVmaze for episode schedules
  • YouTube for trailer thumbnails, and trailers if you choose to play one

These requests contain what's needed to fetch the content, such as the title you searched for and your selected region. Like any internet request, they also reveal your IP address to that service. They do not include your name, your watchlist, or any account details. Each service handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

We do not collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your local library in the app's Settings. Removing the app deletes its data from your device. Data in your iCloud account can be managed in your iCloud settings.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at this URL.
